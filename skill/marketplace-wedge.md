# Marketplace Wedge

## Goal

Help the founder choose one narrow starting market instead of building a generic marketplace.

## Good starting wedges

1. **VRChat / VTuber avatar assets**
   - Strong identity-driven buying.
   - Visual demos look good.
   - Piracy and license confusion are real problems.
   - Buyers may not want crypto friction.

2. **STL miniatures and premium printables**
   - Clear file ownership pain.
   - Personal vs commercial license matters.
   - Easy to show protected delivery and updates.

3. **Indie game props and asset packs**
   - Buyers care about formats, engine compatibility, and support.
   - License clarity matters more than speculation.

4. **Archviz / product visualization assets**
   - Higher ticket.
   - Strong commercial licensing angle.
   - Slower sales cycle, but good for premium positioning.

## Wedge selection questions

Ask:

- Can we contact 50 creators in this niche this week?
- Can we get 5 honest calls?
- Do buyers already pay for these assets?
- Is license confusion or file delivery actually painful?
- Can one demo asset show the whole product story?
- Would Solana receipts increase trust, or just add noise?

## Recommendation logic

If the founder has no existing creator relationships, recommend the niche where outreach is fastest.

If the founder has a beautiful demo but no supply, recommend a curated landing + creator interviews before engineering.

If the founder wants to build for everyone, push back and force a first wedge.

## Output format

Return:

- recommended wedge;
- why this wedge wins now;
- first 20 creator targets by type;
- first 3 asset categories;
- what to validate in interviews;
- what not to build yet.

## Safety boundary

This module is not legal, financial, or security advice. It helps structure founder decisions and prepare better questions for counsel, engineers, and operators.
