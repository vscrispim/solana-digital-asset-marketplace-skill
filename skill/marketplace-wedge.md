# Marketplace and Utility Wedge

## Goal

Help the founder choose one narrow starting market or real-world utility instead of building a generic marketplace or generic NFT platform.

This module is especially useful when a founder says: “Magic Eden is changing, so we should build an alternative.” The right answer is usually not to clone a large NFT marketplace. The right answer is to choose a wedge where ownership, access, media, membership, redemption, or proof creates a real user benefit.

## Good starting wedges

1. **Music or video drops with gated extras**
   - Fans can buy a normal checkout product and optionally receive a Solana receipt or NFT access pass.
   - Useful extras: stems, behind-the-scenes video, Discord access, ticket presale, merch claim.

2. **Event tickets and attendance proofs**
   - Good for universities, hackathons, conferences, local events, and communities.
   - Solana can support ticket proof, attendance badges, post-event perks, and anti-fraud checks.

3. **Education credentials and hackathon certificates**
   - Workshops, bootcamps, campus programs, hackathons, and builder communities need proof of participation.
   - Users care about shareable credentials, not crypto complexity.

4. **Local business passes, coupons, or loyalty claims**
   - Real-world redemption gives the NFT/pass a concrete purpose.
   - Works for restaurants, events, coworking spaces, gyms, and creator merch.

5. **Creator media stores**
   - Photos, presets, templates, design packs, writing, sample packs, and video assets.
   - Buyers need license clarity, protected downloads, and creator trust.

6. **3D / avatar / STL / game asset marketplaces**
   - Still a strong use case, but now treated as one category inside a broader digital media and utility marketplace skill.

## Magic Eden context

Magic Eden is a major NFT marketplace. If it changes strategy, supported chains, fee model, product focus, creator tools, or marketplace UX, founders may look for alternatives.

Do not compete head-on with Magic Eden on generic NFT liquidity. Compete on a sharper job-to-be-done:

- “tickets for campus events with proof of attendance”;
- “music drops with unlockable fan perks”;
- “certificates for hackathon builders”;
- “local coupons with wallet-optional redemption”;
- “creator media licensing with protected files”;
- “3D assets with license receipts and updates.”

## Wedge selection questions

Ask:

- Can we contact 20–50 creators, venues, schools, brands, or communities this week?
- Is there a real benefit beyond “it is an NFT”?
- Does the user need ownership, access, proof, redemption, or a license?
- Can the first demo show a complete real-world journey?
- Can the product work with wallet optional?
- What would Magic Eden or a generic marketplace not solve well for this niche?
- What must be protected: file, stream, ticket QR, credential, coupon, or membership access?

## Recommendation logic

If the founder wants “an NFT marketplace,” force a real utility definition first.

If the founder mentions Magic Eden, ask what user group is underserved by a broad marketplace.

If the founder is in a hackathon, recommend a wedge that can be demoed end-to-end in 60–90 seconds.

If the founder has no partners, recommend creator/media drops or event credentials because outreach is faster.

## Output format

Return:

- recommended wedge;
- why this wedge wins now;
- user benefit beyond speculation;
- NFT/pass/receipt role;
- first 20 partner or creator targets by type;
- first 3 media/access/benefit categories;
- what to validate in interviews;
- what not to build yet.

## Safety boundary

This module is not legal, financial, or security advice. It helps structure founder decisions and prepare better questions for counsel, engineers, and operators.
