# Funding Readiness

## Goal

Prepare a founder to show a Web2.5 marketplace as a serious company, not a speculative crypto demo.

## Demo structure

A good 60–90 second demo shows:

1. a buyer opening a beautiful asset page;
2. the 3D preview working;
3. license tiers explained clearly;
4. checkout/unlock flow;
5. receipt/license page;
6. protected download;
7. optional Solana proof link;
8. creator dashboard or support signal.

## Investor one-pager sections

- Problem;
- initial wedge;
- why now;
- why Solana;
- product workflow;
- GTM plan;
- early validation;
- revenue model;
- roadmap;
- funding use.

## What investors should hear

- “We are not asking buyers to care about crypto first.”
- “We are starting with a curated wedge.”
- “Solana is the proof and settlement layer, not the whole UX.”
- “We know what stays manual until the marketplace earns automation.”

## Output format

Return a demo script, one-pager outline, and data-room checklist.

## Safety boundary

This module is not legal, financial, or security advice. It helps structure founder decisions and prepare better questions for counsel, engineers, and operators.
