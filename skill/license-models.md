# License Models

## Goal

Turn vague “buy this file” marketplace copy into understandable license tiers.

## Starter tiers

### Personal license

For personal use, non-commercial projects, learning, private avatars, private prints, or hobby use.

### Commercial license

For paid projects, client work, monetized videos, game releases, brand campaigns, resale of physical prints, or commercial avatar work.

### Studio / multi-seat license

For teams, agencies, schools, or studios where more than one person needs access.

## What every license brief should include

- asset name and version;
- creator name;
- buyer/account;
- allowed use;
- prohibited use;
- redistribution rule;
- modification rule;
- commercial use rule;
- AI/training use rule if relevant;
- update access;
- refund/support terms;
- license hash;
- counsel review status.

## Red flags

Escalate to counsel if:

- the asset uses brand/IP references;
- the creator cannot prove ownership;
- the marketplace promises royalties or resale rights;
- the buyer wants sublicensing;
- the asset includes scans of real people, places, or protected objects;
- the license claims cross-jurisdiction enforceability without review.

## Output format

Return a plain-English license structure and a counsel-ready list of open questions.

## Safety boundary

This module is not legal, financial, or security advice. It helps structure founder decisions and prepare better questions for counsel, engineers, and operators.
