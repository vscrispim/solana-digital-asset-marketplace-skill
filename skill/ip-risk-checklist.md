# IP Risk Checklist

## Goal

Catch obvious intellectual-property and licensing risks before a marketplace publishes an asset.

## Concrete site/UX checks

On the asset page, make ownership and rights visible in plain language:

- “Created by” creator identity;
- included file formats;
- license tier;
- allowed commercial use;
- redistribution rule;
- version/update history;
- report/takedown path.

## Fast checks

- Does the creator claim original authorship?
- Are there brand logos, characters, celebrities, or copyrighted references?
- Was any part generated from third-party scans or ripped game files?
- Are textures, rigs, sounds, fonts, or HDRIs licensed for resale?
- Does the license allow redistribution of source files?
- Is commercial use allowed?
- Are AI training or dataset uses addressed?
- Is there a takedown/dispute process?

## Risk levels

### Low

Original asset, clean source files, no third-party IP, plain license.

### Medium

Uses third-party components with licenses that need review, or has ambiguous commercial rights.

### High

Fan art, ripped assets, brand references, celebrity likeness, unclear ownership, or creator cannot explain provenance.

## Cybersecurity alerts

- A takedown form can be abused for spam or harassment; rate-limit it and log submissions.
- Do not publish private creator documents used for verification. Store them securely and restrict admin access.
- Beware impersonation: fake creator pages can copy portfolios and trick buyers.

## Counsel handoff

Generate a short brief for legal review when risk is medium/high.

## Output format

Return a risk register with severity, evidence, open questions, and recommendation: publish, hold, revise, or reject.

## Safety boundary

This module is not legal, financial, or security advice. It helps structure founder decisions and prepare better questions for counsel, engineers, and operators.
