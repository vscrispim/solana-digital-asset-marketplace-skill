# Demo Script — Web2.5 Digital Asset Marketplace

## 0–10s — Problem

Creators sell valuable digital files, but buyers still ask: what license did I buy, where is the latest file, and how do I know this is legitimate?

## 10–30s — Asset discovery

Show the curated asset page, 3D preview, formats, creator reputation, and license tiers.

## 30–50s — Purchase and unlock

Show checkout or simulated checkout, then protected download access.

## 50–70s — Receipt

Show the human-readable license and optional Solana proof link.

## 70–90s — Why now

Explain the wedge, creator onboarding, and what metric proves demand next.
