# Example: Threed.CC-style 3D Asset Marketplace

## Input

A founder wants to sell curated 3D assets with Web2 checkout, protected download, and Solana receipts.

## Recommended wedge

Start with VRChat/VTuber avatar assets or premium STL collectibles. Do not start as “all 3D assets.”

## Manual MVP

- 3 creators;
- 5 assets;
- curated pages;
- license tiers;
- simulated or real checkout;
- signed download links;
- receipt hash on Solana devnet/testnet;
- buyer feedback form.

## Success criteria

- 20 creator outreaches;
- 5 interviews;
- 3 creators interested;
- 50 asset page views;
- 5 checkout intent clicks;
- 1 purchase/test.
