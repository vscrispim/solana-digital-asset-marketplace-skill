# Example: Music Drop with Membership and Bonus Content

## Input

An independent artist wants to sell a digital drop with normal checkout, optional NFT/pass, and protected bonus content.

## Recommended wedge

Do not start as a broad music NFT marketplace. Start with one artist drop:

- track/EP;
- bonus stems;
- behind-the-scenes video;
- private listening session;
- merch presale or show discount.

## Flow

`Artist page → Drop detail → Buy/claim → Receipt/pass → Protected bonus content → Fan perk redemption`

## Solana role

- Optional fan pass;
- receipt hash;
- access proof for bonus content;
- future loyalty history.

## What stays off-chain

- full audio/video files;
- buyer email;
- private fan data;
- streaming links.

## Cybersecurity alerts

- Do not expose bonus content URLs before authorization.
- Signed URLs should expire.
- Treat uploaded audio/video archives as untrusted.
- Verify webhooks before granting access.

## Success criteria

- 1 artist;
- 1 drop;
- 100 landing visits;
- 20 checkout starts;
- 5 purchases/claims;
- 3 bonus-content accesses.
